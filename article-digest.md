## Best STAR Story — Regulatory Compliance Under Pressure (Bold Finance)

**Situation:** Critical regulatory compliance deadline — 3 weeks to implement new payment verification requirements mandated by India's financial regulators. Missing the deadline meant losing the operating license. The co-founder was considering cutting corners on edge case handling to hit the date.

**Task:** As Technical PM owning the product roadmap, I was responsible for shipping compliant software on time without compromising payment integrity for 200+ lending partners and 15,000+ active loans.

**Action:** Refused to skip payment edge cases despite time pressure. Mapped every possible failure scenario across the payment flow, identified 23 edge cases needing automated handling, and restructured the sprint to parallelize work. Personally wrote acceptance criteria for each edge case, moved to daily standups, and triaged scope ruthlessly — deferred 4 non-critical features to focus on compliance. Set up Retool dashboard for real-time payment failure monitoring post-deployment.

**Result:** Shipped in 2.5 weeks — half a week ahead of the 3-week deadline — with zero regulatory rejections. The 23 automated edge case handlers saved ~10 hours/week of ops work. The co-founder confirmed it was the right call, and the regulator flagged us as a model implementation during their next audit.
